
PNEUMONIA PREVENTION

General preventive measures include:

1. Staying up to date with recommended vaccines.
2. Washing hands regularly.
3. Avoiding smoking.
4. Avoiding exposure to second-hand smoke.
5. Following respiratory hygiene practices.
6. Maintaining general health and nutrition.

RISK FACTORS:
Certain people may have increased risk:
- Young children
- Older adults
- People with weakened immune systems
- People with chronic medical conditions

PREVENTION NOTE:
Vaccination recommendations depend on age,
health status and individual circumstances.
Consult a qualified healthcare professional.
