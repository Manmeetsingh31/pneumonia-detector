
PNEUMONIA: GENERAL INFORMATION

Pneumonia is an infection that causes inflammation
in the air sacs (alveoli) of one or both lungs.

The alveoli may fill with fluid or pus, making
breathing difficult.

CAUSES:
Pneumonia can be caused by:
1. Bacteria
2. Viruses
3. Fungi

COMMON SYMPTOMS:
- Cough, sometimes with mucus
- Fever
- Chills
- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain during breathing or coughing
- Fatigue

DIAGNOSIS:
Healthcare professionals may use:
- Physical examination
- Medical history
- Chest X-ray
- Blood tests
- Sputum tests

A chest X-ray can help identify abnormalities
but should not be used alone to establish
a definitive diagnosis.

COMPLICATIONS:
Severe pneumonia may cause respiratory failure,
bloodstream infection, or other serious complications.

IMPORTANT:
Pneumonia severity varies between individuals.
This information is educational and not a substitute
for professional medical advice.
