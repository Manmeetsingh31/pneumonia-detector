
NORMAL CHEST X-RAY INFORMATION

A normal chest X-ray generally indicates that
no significant abnormality was identified
in the structures examined.

NORMAL LUNG APPEARANCE:
- Lung fields appear relatively clear.
- No obvious focal consolidation is identified.
- No significant pleural effusion is observed.
- The heart size may appear within expected limits.

IMPORTANT:
A normal chest X-ray does not guarantee
that a person has no medical condition.

Image quality, patient history and clinical
symptoms must also be considered.

Machine learning predictions may be incorrect
and should not replace radiologist interpretation.
